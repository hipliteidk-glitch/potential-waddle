Your ship has no engine you can steer. It drifts on a current that only gets faster. The one thing you control is space itself — hold to tear open a gravity well and the ship falls toward it; invert it to shove the ship away. Wells burn charge, so learn to pulse them. Aiming means leading your own momentum.
